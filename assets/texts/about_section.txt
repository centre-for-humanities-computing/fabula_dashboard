This web-app can process text and calculate a large number of metrics. The metrics will then be visualised as points on a distribution.
The distributions are the result of the project Fabula-NET having calculated the exact same metrics on a large corpus of books,
amounting to around 10,000 books. Visualising the metrics as points on a distribution makes it easy to compare your text to the corpus
of Fabula-NET.

&nbsp; 
### Instructions
To get started, you can either upload a file or just write something in the box below the subheading "Write Text" and click "Submit".
If you choose to upload a file, it must be a .txt or .docx file. 
The web-app will then process your text and display it afterwards.
For short texts it will only take a couple of seconds to process, while for full books it can take a couple of minutes.
After processing multiple tabs will appear. Each tab shows a category of metrics calculated from the text. It is important to mention
that none of the texts are saved, but will only be retained as long as your browsing session is active. All progress is deleted upon
leaving the site or clicking refresh.

&nbsp; 
### Languages
Currently only Danish and English text processing are available. For Danish processing, only Stylometrics and Sentiment metrics 
are available, while for English there are also Readability metrics.

&nbsp; 
### Sentiment Analysis
Sentiment analysis is the process of analysing text and determining the "tone" or "valence" of the text. Often to determine whether
the tone is positive, negative, or neutral. There are many different approaches to perform sentiment analysis. On this web-app, 
4 options are available when doing English text processing: `afinn`, `Vader`, `Syuzhet`, and an average of `Vader` and `Syuzhet`. 
The only sentiment analysis method implemented for Danish is a dictionary approach implemented with the package `afinn`.

&nbsp;
Most of the metrics for sentiment analysis only makes sense, if more than 20 sentences are present in the text. Furthermore, the
project of Fabula-NET have used `Vader`for most of their calculations, so to compare with their large corpus, you will have to
limit yourself to using `Vader`.

&nbsp; 
### Comparison Groups
The corpus of Fabula-NET have been divided into subgroups. The average value for each subgroup can be visualised alongside the
value of your text.
